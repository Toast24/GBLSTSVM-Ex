# Minority safeguard counterfactual (read-only)

Hypothetical split gates on frozen recursive KMeans(k=2) (rho=0.95, min_samples_per_ball=1, del_ball num_data=2).

Actual granulation, Topo, CW, and GBLSTSVM are **not** modified. Safeguards do not use classifier or topology performance.

Methods:

- **M0** — baseline: accept every split that meets the purity rule.
- **M1** — reject if the smaller child has n<2 and contains global-minority samples.
- **M2** — reject if smaller child n≤3 and minority_fraction_small_child ≥ 0.5.
- **M3** — reject if parent_n≤4 and minority_fraction_small_child ≥ 0.5.

A rejected proposal leaves the parent unsplit (leaf), so later children of that branch are never created. KMeans(k=2) is still called to evaluate the hypothetical split.

Runs: **204** (51 dataset×seed pairs × 4 methods).

## Verdict

M3 is the best granulation-only tradeoff among M1-M3: mean minority survival 0.940 (M0 0.583, d=+0.3569) with mean balls 123.1 (M0 88.8). M1/M2 can reject a peel from a large mixed parent and freeze that parent, which collapses recursive granulation even when surviving ball count rises. M3 only fires on parent_n<=4. Ranking uses survival, collapse, and whether rejections hit large parents; Topo/GBLSTSVM are unused.

Comparisons:

- M1: d_min_surv=+0.4171, relative ball cut=-0.322, relative proposed-split cut=0.205, collapse_runs=1, frac_rej_parent_n>4=0.165, EXCESSIVE granulation halt.
- M2: d_min_surv=+0.4171, relative ball cut=0.121, relative proposed-split cut=0.429, collapse_runs=3, frac_rej_parent_n>4=0.579, EXCESSIVE granulation halt.
- M3: d_min_surv=+0.3569, relative ball cut=-0.370, relative proposed-split cut=0.096, collapse_runs=0, frac_rej_parent_n>4=0.000, no large-parent halt.

Ranking rule: Penalize collapse (n_balls<2 or missing class) and rejections with parent_n>4. Then rank by minority-survival gain and by keeping proposed-split volume. Ball-count increase from fewer del_ball deletions is not treated as a cost. Safeguard thresholds were not tuned.

Useful retained-style splits (both children n>=2, not a small minority peel) are not the rejected mass under M1-M3. The risk is freezing a large mixed parent when the first KMeans peel is a tiny minority child. M3 cannot do that.
